Each site has its own tab (keepalive-<site>) that CWeb keeps in the background and checks on its own. Signed out: sign in on that tab in the CWeb window. To add a site, tell any Claude session "keep <site> alive". Copy puts a site's script on the clipboard to paste into Add on another machine. Saved in the data folder\keepalive, never in git.
